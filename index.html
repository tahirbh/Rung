OFFICIAL VIP CLUB RULES & MULTI-DEVICE GUIDE

How to Play RUNG (Court Piece)

1. 52-Card Deal & Trump Selection

Played by 4 players in two partnerships (Seat 1 & Seat 3 vs. Seat 2 & Seat 4). Each player first receives 5 cards so the Trump Chooser can inspect their hand and call the Trump Suit (Rung), then the remaining cards are dealt to complete 13 cards each.

The winning team of a game chooses trump in the next game. If a team wins a 13-point Court, trump selection shifts to the lead winner's partner!

2. Senior Lead-Holder & Table Stacking

Played cards stay stacked on the table until a player who holds the lead wins a consecutive hand on their own lead! If your partner leads Q♦, an opponent plays K♦, and you overtake with A♦, you become Senior even over your own partner — cards stay on the table and you must lead and win the next hand yourself to sweep all stacked points!

Whenever an opponent cuts with trump or overtakes the lead, cards and points keep stacking on the table, building massive hype until a consecutive win sweeps the entire pile!

3. Kick Bot & Invite Partner (Table ID + Player ID)

The Table Owner can click Kick Bot (Partner) on Seat 3 (or any bot seat) at any time during a live game. Share the 6-character Table ID with your friend; they enter the Table ID + their Player ID on the Home tab, and upon approval they immediately take over that bot's seat and cards!
